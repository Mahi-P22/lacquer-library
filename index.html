How to post it
  1. Tap Copy caption.
  2. Open the Instagram app and start a new post with your nail photo.
  3. Long-press the caption box and tap Paste. The brand tags are already in it.

Instagram only lets approved business apps post for you, so copy and paste is the way. It takes about five seconds.
